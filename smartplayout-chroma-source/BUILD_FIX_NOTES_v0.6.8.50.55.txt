Log (7) confirms compilation and self-contained publish succeeded with 0
errors. The unique verification pipe connected, but PING validation failed.

Windows PowerShell's ConvertTo-Json representation of a DateTimeOffset is not
guaranteed to match the ISO-8601 string required by System.Text.Json when it
deserializes ModuleCommand.CreatedUtc. PING, REGISTER_ROUTE and SHUTDOWN now
send CreatedUtc explicitly in round-trip ISO-8601 format ("O"). PING failure
also records the complete raw worker reply for actionable diagnostics.
